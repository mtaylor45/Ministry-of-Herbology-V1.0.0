# The Naturalist's Journal — the journal

Owned by the journal. Landed.

| Route                   | What it is                                        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `/journal`              | The book view: one leaf per plant in the register |
| `/journal/:specimen_id` | One plant's leaf, plus its field notes            |

The previous version of this file said the directory was deliberately empty and
that the app's screens' Specimen page "links here and gets a 404, which is the honest state of
the feature". It was, and this is the releases that retires it.

## The one thing to read before changing anything here

**A generated plate must never be able to pass as a historical botanical
illustration.**

The house style asks for a _"nineteenth-century botanical plate, hand-coloured
lithograph… aged cream paper"_. That is the right aesthetic for this book and it
is also, unlabelled, a **fabricated historical artefact** — an image that looks
like a scan of a plate from 1840 and is not one.

`origin: generated` in the payload is not enough on its own, because a field a
developer can see is not a label a reader is given. the design settled the
analogous question for task certainty — _next to the instruction, not behind a
tooltip_ — and a book view's "next to the instruction" is **on the plate**. So:

- `ProvenanceNote.svelte` renders beside every plate, always, never collapsed
  and never a hover. A generated plate's note is the loud one: heavier border,
  an icon, and the words "Illustration generated by software — not a historical
  plate". Status is not carried by colour alone — the words do the
  work and the colour agrees with them.
- the same sentence is inside the plate's `alt` text (`altTextFor` in
  `plates.ts`), so a reader who cannot see the note is not told _less_ than one
  who can. An honest-looking image with an inaccessible caption is the gap the design refused to leave.
- a generated plate carries **no** licence and **no** attribution. Both are
  null by construction (`workers/plates/domain.py`), and nothing in this
  directory fills them in. `ProvenanceNote` says out loud that there is no
  collection or artist to credit, rather than leaving a blank credit line under
  a nineteenth-century-looking picture — a blank byline there reads as
  "uncredited antique", which is the misreading the note exists to prevent.

## The book pages through plants, not plates

the earlier exit criterion is _"each specimen has an approved plate; the book view
pages through all"_. That is a coverage target, and a coverage target is the
classic reason to lower a bar.

A book built from the plates it happens to have would page through nine leaves
and read as complete while three plants were missing from it. So `buildPages()`
makes a leaf per **specimen**, and a plant with no plate gets a leaf that names
it and says there is none. Same decision as `MorningRounds.unscheduled[]`: _a plant with no task is indistinguishable from a plant that needs
nothing_, and a plant missing from a book of plates is indistinguishable from a
plant nobody owns.

Three states, and the middle one is the one worth defending:

| State     | What the reader sees                                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `plate`   | An approved plate, with its provenance note                              |
| `waiting` | The plate, **marked as not yet accepted**, with the approval outstanding |
| `absent`  | the maintainers named blank leaf                                         |

`waiting` is shown rather than hidden because hiding it makes the book look
thinner than the work is, and auto-approving it to fill the leaf is the one
thing this release must not do.

`coverageSentence()` prints the real numbers above the book. It is a reported
figure, never a rounded one.

## Two things it deliberately does not know

- **Why** a plant has no plate. The pipeline knows (every
  `PlateOutcome` carries a reason), and the frozen contract has nowhere to put
  it, so `absenceSentence()` says what is true from here — there is no plate —
  and stops. Guessing "nothing was found" when the truth might be "the volume
  is unmounted" would be the invented answer. Escalated in the pull request.
- **Which member is approving**, beyond taking the first in the household. The
  contract has no approval endpoint at all, so the one this talks to is itself
  an escalation (`api.ts`). A proper member picker belongs with the contract
  line.

## Accessibility notes

- every plate has real alt text saying what it is _and_ whether it was
  generated;
- page turning works from the keyboard — the two controls are real buttons in
  the tab order, and ← / → turn the leaf. The key handler ignores events from
  inputs and textareas, so writing a field note cannot turn the page out from
  under the writer;
- the folio line (`Leaf 4 of 12`) is `aria-live="polite"`, so a turn is
  announced rather than discovered;
- the page-turn fade is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`, in the
  stylesheet rather than in script, so it holds whether or not the component
  hydrates;
- colour comes from the design system's tokens only. There is no colour
  literal in this directory.
